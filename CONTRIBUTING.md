## Contributing

Contributions to **Good Practices in R** are welcome, including corrections, exercises, learner feedback, and reviews of proposed changes.
This lesson is at the `pre-alpha` stage, so feedback on its scope, structure, and unfinished material is particularly useful.
Please follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

### Contributor agreement

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be redistributed under the applicable [project licences](LICENSE.md).
Keep attribution and licence notices when adapting material from other sources.

### Report a problem or suggest an improvement

- [Open an issue][repo-issues] to report an error, suggest an exercise, or describe something that was unclear when learning or teaching the lesson.
  Include the episode or page, the relevant text or code, and any error messages or steps needed to reproduce a problem.
- Comment on existing issues or review a [pull request][repo-pulls].
  Feedback from learners and first-time contributors is welcome.

### Submit a change

Before making substantial changes, please [open an issue][repo-issues] to discuss your proposal.
Wait for maintainer feedback before starting work or submitting a large pull request. 
This helps avoid duplicated effort and ensures changes align with the lesson's scope.

Small corrections, such as typos or broken links, can be submitted directly as pull requests.

1. Fork the [repository][repo] (if needed) and create a branch.
2. Make focused changes in `episodes/`, `learners/`, or `instructors/`. 
   Preserve Workbench markup and use one sentence per line.
3. Follow the build instructions in the [README](README.md). **Check the local preview**, including formatting, links, and exercises, before marking your PR ready for review.
4. Open a pull request against `main`, describe your changes and testing, and address review feedback and automated check failures.
