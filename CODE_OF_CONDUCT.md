---
title: "Code of Conduct"
---

As contributors, maintainers, and instructors of **Good Practices in R**, we are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and supportive learning environment.

This lesson uses **The Carpentries lesson template** and adopts the behavioural standards outlined in [The Carpentries Code of Conduct][coc]. However, it is maintained by UCL Advanced Research Computing (ARC) and is not currently an official Carpentries lesson.

We expect all participants and contributors to:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints, backgrounds, and experiences.
- Be courteous and considerate towards others.
- Give and receive constructive feedback respectfully.
- Refrain from harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and disruptive behaviour.

## Unacceptable behaviour

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or behaviour that makes others feel unsafe or excluded. 
This includes:

- Offensive, discriminatory, or exclusionary comments, jokes, or personal attacks.
- Threats, bullying, or unwanted sexual attention or physical contact.
- Repeated disruption of teaching, discussions, or collaborative activities.
- Continuing unwanted interactions after being asked to stop.
- Sharing private communications, photographs, or recordings without consent.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour during an ARC-delivered training session, we encourage you to speak to one of the instructors or helpers, either during the session or privately afterwards, if you feel comfortable doing so.

Alternatively, or if you prefer not to approach the instructors, you can report concerns directly to ARC.education@ucl.ac.uk.

Concerns relating to interactions within this repository may also be reported to the same address.

Reports will be handled sensitively and in accordance with relevant UCL procedures.
Organisers may take appropriate action, including asking individuals to leave an activity.

[coc]: https://docs.carpentries.org/policies/coc/
